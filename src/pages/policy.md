# Content policy

*Draft. Review this page with your university before the library goes public.*

## What can be shared

- Your own notes, summaries, lab write-ups and projects.
- Material your instructor has explicitly said may be shared.
- Openly licensed material, such as Creative Commons content or open textbooks.
- Past exam papers, only where the university permits it.

## What cannot be shared

- Pirated or scanned copies of textbooks and other paid material.
- Leaked exams, or exams that have not been sat yet.
- Solutions to graded assignments that are still open.
- Anything that contains other people's personal information.
- Anything the owner has asked to have removed.

## How it works

The library does not host files. Each entry links to a file kept by whoever shared it, and every entry says why it may be shared. A moderator reviews every submission before it appears.

## Taking something down

If you own something listed here and want it removed, report it. We remove first and discuss afterwards, and we aim to act within 48 hours. Use the "report a problem" link on the entry, or contact: **(add a contact email before launch)**.

## Privacy

The site itself has no accounts, sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Contributor names are optional.
